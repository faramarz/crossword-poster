# Getting help

Where to go depends on what you need.

- **I am stuck, or I want to know how to do something.** Ask in
  [Discussions (Q&A)](https://github.com/faramarz/crossword-poster/discussions/categories/q-a). Say what you ran and what
  you saw. Please do not paste real or private clues, names or answers: made-up ones that behave the same way are enough.
- **Something is not working.** Run `crossword-poster doctor` first, then read the
  [troubleshooting guide](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md). It covers the common problems: Chromium, "command not found", answers
  that do not fit, and printing.
- **I am new to all of this.** The [beginner guide](docs/GUIDE.md) goes from collecting clues to the party.
- **I found a bug.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/faramarz/crossword-poster/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
  It asks for the output of `crossword-poster doctor`, which makes most problems quick to find.
- **I have an idea.** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/faramarz/crossword-poster/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml),
  or start a conversation in Discussions.
- **I found a security problem.** Do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This is a hobby project that one person looks after in their spare time, so replies can take a few days.
